3. Where it's stored and who processes it

We rely on a small number of infrastructure providers, each bound by its own privacy terms:

  • Firebase Authentication (Google) — handles sign-in; see Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Cloudflare — hosts the Service (Pages, Workers, D1 database) and runs Turnstile, our bot check on the payment form; see Cloudflare's Privacy Policy.
  • YouTube and Google Drive — host recorded lectures and study materials you may open from inside the Service; Google's own privacy terms apply to those pages.

We do not use these providers' data for any purpose beyond operating the Service.

4. Children's data

The Service is used by school students as young as Class 5, who are minors. We collect only the profile and activity data described in Section 2 — the minimum needed to run classes, tests and enrollment — and we do not use a minor's data for advertising or behavioural profiling.

We encourage parents and guardians to be involved in a student's account. A parent or guardian may contact us using the details in Section 8 to review, correct or request deletion of a student's data.

5. How long we keep it

We keep account and learning-activity data for as long as the account is active, so your history (results, enrollments, materials) stays available to you. If you ask us to delete your account, we remove your profile and personal data within a reasonable time, except where a record must be kept longer — for example, payment records needed for accounting or dispute resolution.

6. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you (or a parent/guardian acting for a minor) can ask us to:

  • See the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate profile information
  • Delete your account and associated personal data
  • Tell you which third parties your data has been shared with

Send these requests to the contact in Section 8. We will respond within a reasonable time.

7. Security

Sign-in is handled entirely by Firebase Authentication — we never see or store your password. Every request to our servers is checked against your signed-in identity before any data is returned. Test questions and answer keys are never sent to your device before you submit; scoring happens on our servers. No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable technical steps to protect what we hold.
