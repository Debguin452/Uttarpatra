What a student gets

Everything stays scoped to the batches you're actually enrolled in.

BatchesA catalogue filtered by your class and exam, with the ones you've joined always one tap away — archived batches stay visible too.
Lectures & materialsRecorded classes and notes, organised by batch, not scattered across group chats.
Timed testsA real exam clock, a question palette, mark-for-review, one attempt — scored the moment you submit.
DoubtsAsk a question against your batch. It reaches the teachers assigned to it.
